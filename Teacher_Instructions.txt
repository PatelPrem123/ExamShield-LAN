1. HOW TO START THE SERVER & TEACHER CONTROL CENTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "ExamShield_Teacher_Server.exe" inside this folder.
2. The server initializes in under 2 seconds using the embedded portable runtime.
   - NO Node.js or third-party software installation required on the Teacher PC!
3. The Teacher Control Center opens automatically in a dedicated application window.
4. Note the host URL displayed at the top:
   Example: http://192.168.29.139:3000
5. Write or project this URL on the whiteboard for students to access.


2. TEACHER AUTHENTICATION & SECURITY PIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Default Security PIN: admin123
- All teacher operations (changing exam settings, uploading papers, unfreezing
  candidates, and downloading grade sheets) are protected by this PIN.
- Changing PIN: Click "Change PIN" in the top bar, enter current PIN, and set a
  new secure PIN.


3. EXAM CONFIGURATION & MARKING SCHEMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Navigate to the "Exam Settings" tab in the Teacher Control Center:
- Exam Title: e.g. "Term 1 Final Examination - Computer Science"
- Duration (Minutes): Overall time limit (e.g. 60 minutes)
- Passing Percentage: e.g. 70%
- Questions Per Topic: Balance of questions drawn per subject topic (e.g. 15)
- Competitive Marking Mode (+4 / -1):
  * When ENABLED: +4 marks for correct answer, -1 mark penalty for wrong answer,
    0 marks for unattempted questions. Percentage is calculated out of max possible marks.
  * When DISABLED (Standard Mode): +1 mark for correct, 0 for wrong/unattempted.
- Per-Question Timer:
  * When enabled, candidates have a fixed time (e.g. 60s) per question.
  * Note: Mutually exclusive with Competitive Mode (competitive exams give students
    flexibility across the entire duration).


4. QUESTION BANK MANAGEMENT (DOCX IMPORT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Uploading Questions: Click "Question Bank", then "Upload DOCX".
- Select one or more Microsoft Word (.docx) files. Sample papers are provided
  in the "sample-papers/" folder.
- Supported Question Formats in DOCX:
  * Single Choice: Answer: A  (or Answer: A (single))
  * Multiple Choice: Answer: A, C  (or Answer: A, C (multiple))
  * Embedded Diagrams & Images in questions or options are fully supported!
  * Explanations / Solutions are automatically excluded from student view.
- Manual Question Creator: Add individual custom questions with embedded diagrams directly
  from the teacher UI.
- Export Question Bank: Click "Export DOCX" to download your active question bank
  as a clean, printable Word document.


5. MULTI-BATCH MANAGEMENT (COHORT ISOLATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Designed for computer labs hosting multiple student groups across the day.
- Create new batches (e.g. "BATCH-01", "BATCH-02", "AFTERNOON_SHIFT").
- Active Batch: Only candidates assigned to the active batch can take the exam.
- Name Collision Protection: Candidates with the same name in different batches
  remain strictly isolated with independent sessions, papers, and grades.
- Filter Dashboard: Filter candidate leaderboard by specific batch or view "All Batches".


6. REAL-TIME PROCTORING & ANTI-CHEAT SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Zero-Tolerance Focus Detection: If a candidate minimizes the exam window,
  presses Alt+Tab, or switches to another browser tab/app:
  1. The candidate's screen is IMMEDIATELY FROZEN with a Red Warning Banner.
  2. A RED ALERT notification sounds and highlights on the Teacher Dashboard.
  3. The violation timestamp and reason are permanently logged in the audit trail.
- 1-Click Candidate Unfreeze:
  * The invigilator speaks with the candidate to investigate the incident.
  * Click the green "Unfreeze" button on the student's dashboard card to restore
    their exam attempt with all previously answered questions intact.
- Force Submit: Invigilators can override and submit an individual candidate's paper
  at any time, or click "End Exam for All" at the end of the session.


7. ANSWER KEY REVIEW & POST-EXAM RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Safe Scorecards: After submitting, students immediately see their Net Score,
  Right Answers, Wrong Answers, Skipped Questions, accurate Time Spent, and Violations.
- Instant Celebration: Celebratory confetti triggers automatically as soon as the
  score countup finishes, regardless of pass or fail score.
- Answer Key Lockdown: By default, detailed question-by-question answer keys
  are LOCKED to prevent candidates from leaking answers to adjacent students.
- Publishing Answer Keys:
  * Click "Publish Answers to All" to unlock the solution key for the entire cohort.
  * Or click "Allow Review" on individual students for one-on-one reviews.


8. DATA PORTABILITY: SCHOOL PC ➔ HOME LAPTOP TRANSFER (NEW in v2.5.0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Want to review student papers or check grades at home on your personal laptop?
ExamShield provides seamless 1-Click Database Portability:

[AT SCHOOL (TEACHER PC)]
1. Open the Teacher Control Center.
2. In the "Results" tab or "Exam Settings" tab, click the blue "💾 Backup DB" button.
3. A complete snapshot file (e.g. "ExamShield_Backup_2026-11-03.db") will download.
4. Copy this file onto your USB Pen Drive.

[AT HOME (PERSONAL LAPTOP)]
1. Open ExamShield LAN on your home laptop.
2. Click the orange "📥 Restore DB" button.
3. Select the backup file from your USB Pen Drive and confirm.
4. Instantly, all student papers, batches, and question banks will appear on your
   laptop exactly as they were in school!

10. PERMANENT AUDIT HISTORY ARCHIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Even if you reset the exam or clear live sessions for a new batch, all historical

- history.bat : Double-click to view a interactive colored console viewer of all past exams.
- history.txt : Human-readable chronological audit ledger.
- history.csv : Cumulative spreadsheet of all candidates across all dates and batches.


11. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Students cannot open the exam URL on their devices.
A: Ensure all candidate devices are connected to the SAME Wi-Fi or LAN switch.
   Check Windows Defender Firewall: click "Allow access" if prompted on server launch.

Q: A student's laptop battery died or browser closed accidentally.
A: ExamShield features Safe Auto-Reconnection. The student simply re-enters their
   exact name; the system restores their exact questions, answered choices, and timer!

Q: Can candidates inspect source code to find correct answers?
A: No. ExamShield LAN runs 100% server-side validation. Correct answers are completely
   stripped from student network payloads before transmission.


12. DUAL-CHANNEL ANSWER PERSISTENCE & FAULT TOLERANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dual Sync: Every option selection by a student is saved simultaneously via
  WebSocket and non-blocking HTTP REST API (POST /api/student/answer).
- Micro-disconnect Protection: Even if a candidate's Wi-Fi connection drops
  momentarily, choices are saved via HTTP background fallback and synchronized
  upon re-connection.
- Final Submit Safeguard: On clicking "Finish & Submit Exam", the candidate's
  complete answer dictionary is transmitted and verified against the server,
  guaranteeing zero answer loss.
